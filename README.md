### About me

I'm an M.Sc. student in Bioinformatics Engineering at **Amirkabir University of Technology** in Tehran, where I also did my bachelor's in Biomedical Engineering.

My thesis is on **classifying stem cell differentiation from microscopy images**. Fluorescent markers can tell you which lineage a cell has committed to, but the dye kills the cell. So I'm training a model to predict those markers directly from plain brightfield images — non-destructive, and you can keep watching the same cell over time.

### 🧪 Projects

**[Self-Supervised Blood Cell Classification for Leukemia Detection](https://github.com/Alihaatefi/convnextv2-fcmae-blood-cells)**
Labelled data is scarce in medical imaging, so I pretrained a model by masking 60% of pixels at random and forcing it to reconstruct cellular structure before ever seeing a label. ConvNeXt V2 + FCMAE, built from scratch in PyTorch. I also ran the controls the original course project had skipped — two of them went against the result I was hoping for, and both are still in the README.

**[Skin Cancer Detection and Classification](https://github.com/Alihaatefi/skin-cancer-cnn-benchmark)**
Seven CNN architectures benchmarked under one protocol for malignant vs. benign dermoscopic lesions, with cross-validation, threshold-free metrics and bootstrap intervals. Outperformed the DenseNet baseline from the literature under identical conditions.

**[Telerehabilitation Hand Therapy](https://github.com/Alihaatefi/telerehab-hand-therapy)**
Webcam-based hand rehabilitation that calibrates to each patient's own range of motion, with clinician-facing progress reports. B.Sc. final project.

**[Encoder–Decoder Model for Epileptic Seizure Detection](https://github.com/Alihaatefi/eeg-eye-state-bss)**
A deep encoder–decoder network for detecting seizures in EEG, with PCA and ICA for preprocessing and source separation.
